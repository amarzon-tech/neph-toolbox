About this draft

Nephrology Toolbox is a clinician-facing collection of formulas used in nephrology. It is a draft for review by Dr Amar Damodar (Dr D). It is not for clinical use until he has signed off on every formula.

How it is built

  • A static app that works offline (installable PWA). After the first load, it does not need a network.
  • No login, no ads, no analytics, and no data collection. Patient inputs stay in memory on this device and are never stored.
  • Favorites and Recents remember only calculator names, on this device. Your US/SI preference is also kept locally. Nothing leaves the device.
  • Every formula is taken from its original or guideline source. The equation, citation, and limitations are in “About this formula,” and again in CONTENT_REVIEW.md for sign-off.

Privacy

This app makes no network calls for calculations and does not send, save, or share patient data. Citation links (DOI, PubMed) open only if you choose to follow them.
